## 1. McKee's Five-Step Scene Analysis Examples

### *Casablanca* Linen Stall (Mid-Act Climax)
- Conflict: Rick's desire "to win Ilsa back"; Ilsa's "to protect their past affair while moving forward with her present life." Value = Love; opens on a negative charge leaning slightly positive.
- Beats: 1 Approach / Ignore; 2 Protect / Reject intimacy ("You're deceived"); 3 Apologize (pedantic phrasing masking awkwardness) / Reject again; 4 Make excuses (alcohol) / Fourth rejection—Arab merchant "sells" Rick to Ilsa via price-cutting; 5 Foot in the door (self-evident question) / Crack open ("Yes"); 6 On his knees / Demand more; 7 Make her feel guilty (extra train tickets) / Turn the tables and make him feel guilty; 8 Say farewell ("Remember Paris isn't Casablanca") / Refuse to react; 9 Accuse her of cowardice / Accuse him of foolishness; 10 Sexual overture ("I'll be right above the cafe waiting for you") / Hide face beneath hat brim; 11 Accuse her of being a whore / **Crush hope with revelations** ("Laszlo is my husband, and was when I met you in Paris"—half-truth).
- Ending Value: Sudden shift from hopeful positive to a far heavier negative than anticipated. Each exchange surpasses the last, with escalating stakes and mounting willpower; the gap opens in the middle of Beat 11; turns on a **revelation**.

### *Through a Glass Darkly* Attic Scene (Bergman)
- Karin (hallucinatory schizophrenic) drives the action, desire "to see God and have the men welcome Him together"; counterforce: husband Martin + her own subconscious. Opening value: Hope.
- 20 Beats: Plan escape / Mask guilt -> Flee toward God / Aid her -> Pray / Rush to find -> Prepare epiphany / Observe her madness -> Restrain / Defend belief -> Deny God / Stand firm -> Order departure / Fall back -> Pull into ritual / Yield (kneel) -> Caress / Break away -> Pray with whole heart / **"God" announces arrival** -> Manifest / Welcome -> **Attack / Shake off** -> Attempt rape / Resist -> Flee / Intercept -> Sedative / Yield -> Warn Minus / Listen in silence—"The door opened, and God was a spider." Closet door opening on its own + helicopter descending = meaningful coincidence fused into an intensely emotional center. Turns on **action** rather than revelation.

### *Kramer vs. Kramer* French Toast
- Three values (self-confidence, son's trust, family survival) across three dimensions (inner doubt / son's tears / alien kitchen world) turning simultaneously = complex scene.

### *Chinatown* Act II Climax Beat by Beat (Subjective POV Demonstration)
Gittes rehearses "I must stay calm" -> Sees house and flies into rage (gap) -> Rams door -> "Hold on" -> Cantonese cursing -> Evelyn "Thank God it's Jack" -> "Where is that girl" -> Silver cigarette case / lighter / glasses wrapped in handkerchief -> "That's where he drowned" -> Grabs her then lets go -> "She's my sister / She's my daughter" slapping loop -> "She's my sister and my daughter" -> "Did he rape you?" shakes head (crucial rewrite: cut explanation of father's mental collapse, preserving Cross's cruelty and testing Gittes's love) -> "Those weren't Hollis's glasses, he didn't wear bifocals" (gap closes) -> 1712 Alameda Street. Step outside subjectivity and ask: "What is the opposite of that reaction?"

## 2. Syd Field: Sequences and Scene Examples

- *Network* "Mad as Hell" Sequence: Beale walks through rain into building -> Control room -> Speech "I'm a human being, goddammit!" -> Diana checks affiliates "Is anybody shouting?" -> Max's daughter opens window and hears the whole city screaming -> "We've lost control of the situation."
- "Homecoming" Sequence: Determine the idea first, then flesh out content (military adviser on plane / family waking up / cross-cutting / airport military band / reunion).
- *Silver Streak* Train Compartment Scene: Tape recorder, champagne, "treating azaleas like nasturtiums," dead body hanging outside the window—functions simultaneously as Plot Point I.
- Contrast Method: *Divorce Italian Style*—church forbids divorce -> find wife a lover -> kill wife with righteous justification; Neil Simon's "contradictory wills" producing sparks.

## 3. Richard Walter: Location Substitution Exercises

| Original Scene | Alternative | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Detective inspector confronts superior in office | Swimming pool: Boss does laps on doctor's orders, detective shouts along poolside, chlorinated water splashes onto $600 alligator shoes | Status humiliation vs. patient maneuvering, rank-and-file cop vs. boss who can afford $600 shoes |
| Radical student meets father in restaurant | Car wash: Daughter wears work coveralls wiping windshield | Audience stays awake |
| Man and woman meet by water cooler | Parking lot tire change: "Did the women's movement happen just so sisters could ruin nails and shred stockings wrenching lug nuts?" | Conflict acted out through physical action (jack, hubcap, spare tire) |
| Female diner orders two main courses at brothers' restaurant | Chef younger brother refuses to cook | Metaphor for commercial vs. artistic conflict |
| Telephone exposition | *Atlantic City*: Pretending to dial in phone booth to retrieve package from top of payphone; *The Golden Years*: Ambient din while aunt slips on wedding dress; *Hook*: Throwing telephone into snow | Phone becomes the center of physical action |

Walter's Margin Notation Symbols: **$?** (Is this line worth the audience's money?); **3 Strikes** (Does it have purpose? Does it have value? Is it the best way?—*The Lion King* wildebeest stampede achieves three goals in one stroke); **Mood spelled backward is Doom** (painting mood never replaces story); **Notnot** (never write "there is no answer" or "does nothing"); **Too Straight-Line** (cause followed immediately by effect = straight line; jump across like a chessboard); **Conk** (concrete); **Ess. Det. Only / SIFYN** (Save It For Your Novel).

## 4. Lu Jun: Suspense and Retardation Models

### Seven Suspenses in *The Silly Son-in-Law Sells Cloth*
Are the guest's words true? -> Can the silly son-in-law be found? -> What happens if the conversation is cut short and no money is collected? -> What does Master Wan mean by sending the bamboo tube? -> Why pack bags to return to her parents' home? -> Is reunion possible within seven days? -> "The first spouse is best"—a change of heart.

### Retardation in *A Night at an Inn*
Sniggers panics upon returning to the room, desperately begging the "Toff" to take back the ruby—"That ruby, my share of it, I don't want it now." "Nonsense, Sniggers." "You can have my share, Toff." "Are you going to peach on us?" "No, no, I just don't want that ruby." "Did you see the police?" "I swear I saw something I didn't like." "What didn't you like?" (eyes brimming with tears) "Oh Toff, Toff, take it back!"—having thoroughly whetted appetites, a cold, stony footstep sounds; the blind idol groans and gropes, picks up the ruby, and embeds it into its brow; followed by further retardation: the idol exits, but its voice calls out names one by one, and the bandits walk out one after another.

### Five Layers in *When Mr. Leonidas Met the "Reactionaries"*
Lectures wife grandly on revolution -> Imminent storm of physical actions and sound effects -> Convinces wife there is no revolution -> Gunfire, newspapers, flight, packing bags, frantic knocking -> Open door to find total misunderstanding (a bunch of drunks).

### Two Approaches in *The Birthday Banquet Hall*
| Keep Secret | Hand Over the Keys |
|---|---|
| Jiao and Meng enter in mourning dress to announce bad news, shocking the hall—instant effect of "surprise" | The audience, Mu Guiying, and Princess Chai already know before the banquet, forcing smiles and urging Jiao and Meng to take off mourning robes and celebrate together—charm of "interest," the Grand Dowager standing firm and raising her cup with tears held back |

## 6. Lu Jun: Three Criteria for Scene Excellence

- **Conception**: *We Want to Live*—a tiny alcove beside a train tunnel accommodating only two people. When a train comes, someone must perish; Clyde waves, and the train miraculously stops (strike along the rail line).
- **Character**: *The Three Whips*—a mud pit midway up the mountain with three layers of background (guerilla base camp trapped enemy trucks and Eighth Route Army artillery wagons / cadres coming down to the village "eating off individual households" / poor transportation making foreign investors say goodbye) -> Old Uncle Zhao vents his frustration -> fights for dignity -> opens his heart.
- **Conflict**: *The Elevator*—trapped in an elevator needing to relieve oneself: aluminum pot and black trench coat acting as a screen; *The Revolving Restaurant*—picnic on scaffolding overlooking Shanghai; *Zhang the Antique Dealer Borrows a Wife*—crescent city wall and in-laws' home alternating soliloquies line by line; *Sisters Swapping Marriages*—three spatial layers, upstairs shattering a mirror whose dust drifts into a wine cup downstairs.
- **Atmospheric Fabric**: *Du Niang Bridge*—stone bridge + folk song "A carrying pole pointed at both ends" + the tale of Sister Qin breaking ice to cross the river; *The Seventh Tree Is a Magnolia*—theme song repeated three times, origins of Charity Square, tree planter's proudest memory: "The greatest day of my life was joining the Communist Youth League at twenty."

## 7. Wendy Jane Henson: Action Sequences and Transition Standards

- Action blocks read like lists; establish time, season, and weather first to convey atmosphere, followed by location and key physical features; write only what camera can shoot and actor can play; passive voice is a curse ("Joe is led by Paul" -> "Paul leads Joe"); prune adjectives and adverbs; "She stands there thinking" cannot be filmed.
- Criteria for Scene Transitions: Location / time shift, addition or removal of characters, change in dramatic objective; double spacing between final line of dialogue and next heading.
- Sound effects are bolded only in action blocks and written only when the source is off-screen (thunder, ringing phone, dog barking off-screen).
- Bad Version vs. Good Version in *Fresh Cheese*: Inside UN, "For the past hour delegates have been listening to Dash blathering" (backstory, past tense, impressions derived from others' descriptions) -> Moon base collecting samples -> Habitat module, motorcycle jacket, Mozart, flick knife carving a slice, "My God, it's cheese!" -> Inside UN, shaking the cheese, ambassador grabs phone: "Bring grenades and bazookas in ten minutes."
- Bureaucratic Prose Exercise: Sentences <= 10 words for two weeks; eliminate conjunctions, subordinate clauses, adjectives, and adverbs while retaining transitive verbs; "Pam folds clothes in the laundry room. The telephone rings. Pam dashes to the kitchen and lifts the receiver. No answer. Pam hangs up."

## 8. Neill D. Hicks: Scene Description Models

- *The Cabin*: Marty smells a foul stench and climbs out the window hunting for "something dead"; Congressman George fumbles after her wrapped in a wet towel; Marty finds the baby raccoon is alive and wants to be its new mother: "Take your money back, just drive me and it back to the city"—"I drove all this way just to end up with a raccoon!" (exit before conflict is fully resolved).
- Counter-example *The Hotel Room*: Ceiling, Laura Ashley bedsheets, antique dresser, remote control, sound of shaving in bathroom, "It's so nice here, don't you think?" "Did you say something?"—no information conveyed, plot does not advance.
- *The Lighthouse Staircase*: Footsteps spiraling upward, abnormal eyes in the shadows, Martha crouching ferociously, George leaping out the roof hatch, Martha lunging into his arms, fingers sinking into flesh, shoving toward the railing, body heat fogging the glass—setting scenes in visually arresting environments makes things happen instantly.
- Phone Example: George answers phone / Martha (V.O.) "You absolute bastard!" / intercutting—does not infringe on directorial prerogatives.

## 9. Mei Feng: Value Shifts in the Restaurant Scene of *The Man Who Shot Liberty Valance*

Ransom recites the legal code -> Link refuses (Western frontier code gains upper hand) -> Hallie wants to learn to read, immigrant parents laugh with joy (civilization gains positive charge) -> Tom changes into black suit: "Hang a sign and you'd better protect it with a gun" -> Liberty trips Ransom: "The new waitress" (Western frontier code negative charge: violence committing evil) -> Tom's steady voice + Black partner raising shotgun from the kitchen (only violence can subdue violence) -> Ransom scoops up the steak and slams it onto Peabody's table, breaking the standoff -> Liberty shatters bottles and fires wildly (stopping at dropping money and leaving loses no factual information, but this extra stroke highlights that violence harms far more than it protects) -> "A respectable scene triggered over gravy and potatoes" -> "You could have killed him or he could have killed you over an ordinary piece of meat!" -> Enter the kitchen (restaurant = treacherous West, kitchen = solidarity and warmth). Multiple value pairs shift within a single scene; every appearance of the cactus rose marks a key value locus.
